The global transition from fossil fuel to electrified infrastructure is a capital extraction strategy disguised as an environmental imperative. 

Empirical data demonstrates a negligible net reduction in planetary carbon intensity.  

**Here's why:**

* **Localized to Centralized:** Replacing depreciated, localized direct thermal energy networks (e.g., natural gas lines) with centralized utility generation models introduces significant systemic inefficiencies. Transmission line losses alone account for 6-7% of generated electricity. 
* **Gas Turbine Reliance:**  Dispatchable gas turbines, often touted as a bridge fuel, are not carbon-neutral. They emit significant CO2, methane, and nitrous oxide, further exacerbating the climate crisis.
* **Duplicative Infrastructure Debt:**  The transition necessitates massive investments in new transmission lines, substations, and charging infrastructure, creating a cycle of duplicative infrastructure debt. 
* **Guaranteed Return-on-Equity Taxes:**  End-user ratepayers bear the brunt of this capital extraction through guaranteed return-on-equity taxes embedded in electricity prices.
